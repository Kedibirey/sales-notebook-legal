Last updated: October 4, 2026 · Türkçe

Sales Notebook processes only the data needed to let sales reps keep their customer, sales, offer and agenda records; we do not sell your data, use it for advertising or track your behaviour.

What we use data for

  • Creating your account, letting you sign in and keeping your data yours
  • Providing the app's features: customer list, sales and goal tracking, agenda and reminders, team dashboard
  • Managing your trial and subscription
  • Answering support requests and meeting our legal obligations

Where data is stored and who we share it with

Your data is stored on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) through our hosting provider Supabase. We share it only with the parties below, for the stated purpose:

In team mode, the team's owner and managers can see reps' customer, sales and agenda records; reps see only their own. Unless required by law, your data is not shared with anyone else and is never sold.

How long we keep data

  • Your data is kept while your account is open.
  • When you delete your account, your account data and all records of a workspace you use alone are deleted immediately.
  • If you are a member of a team, the customer, sales, offer and agenda records you entered for the team are transferred to the team owner as company records; your personal goals and settings are deleted.
  • Subscription and invoice records may be kept for as long as tax law requires.

Security

  • All communication between the app and the server uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Who can see each record is enforced by access rules at database level.
  • Passwords are stored as irreversible hashes; the app contains no server admin keys.

Your rights and deleting your account

  • Download your data: Settings > My data and account > Download my data gives you all your records as an Excel file.
  • Correction: You can edit your records inside the app.
  • Delete your account: Settings > My data and account > Delete my account permanently deletes your account from inside the app. If you can't access the app, follow the steps on the account deletion page. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account.

Children

Sales Notebook is a business app and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Users in Turkey (KVKK)

For users in Turkey, data is processed under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK). The legal bases are performance of a contract (Art. 5/2-c), legal obligation (Art. 5/2-ç) and legitimate interest (Art. 5/2-f). Transfers abroad (Supabase in Germany, RevenueCat in the USA, Apple and Google) are made with one of the appropriate safeguards set out in Art. 9 of the Law. Your rights under Art. 11 KVKK and how to apply are set out in the Turkish version; applications are answered free of charge within 30 days.

Customer data you enter in the app

The customer details you enter (name, phone, address, tax number and so on) are your customers' personal data. For this data you, or the company you work for, are the controller; Sales Notebook only stores it on your instructions as a processor.

  • It is your responsibility to have a lawful basis under the applicable law (for example GDPR or KVKK) for entering this data and to inform your customers with your own privacy notice.
  • Sales Notebook stores this data only to provide the service; we never contact your customers, use the data for another purpose or share it.
  • You can meet a customer's deletion or correction request by editing or deleting the record in the app.
  • If you work in a team, customer records belong to your company and can be seen by the team's owner and managers.

Changes and contact
